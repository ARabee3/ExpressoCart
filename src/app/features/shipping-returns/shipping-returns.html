Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

We strive to deliver your products as quickly and safely as possible. Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation.

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days
  • International Shipping: 7-14 business days

We offer free standard shipping on all domestic orders over $50.

Returns & Exchanges

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, we gladly accept returns within 30 days of delivery.

  • Items must be in original, unwashed condition with tags attached.
  • Custom or personalized items are non-refundable.
  • Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of receiving the return.
